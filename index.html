Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between lighting design, programming and operation?

Lighting design defines the intent: technical drawings, lighting plot, choice and placement of fixtures, colours and movement. Programming builds the show on the console, with palettes, cues and sequences timed to the music. Operation is the live control on the night of the event. RayVo Studio covers all three, from site survey to strike.

Can we see the show before the event?

Yes. We produce technical drawings and lighting plots, then 3D renders of the show: you see the layout, the beams and the moods before load-in, and you validate the choices with us.

Does a nightclub need a lighting design?

A lighting design gives the room its visual identity and avoids the “catalogue of effects” look. It builds on the architecture of the venue and the position of the DJ and the crowd, then serves as the basis for mood programs adapted to the different moments of the night.

What information should we prepare for a first conversation?

The type of event, the venue, the date, the number of stages, an indicative budget and, if you have it, the technical specification of the room or stage. Even if incomplete, a first message is enough to get started.

How do I ask for a quote?

Write to us at contact@rayvostudio.com describing your event or your venue: type of stage, date, place, number of stages. We get back to you to clarify the need.
