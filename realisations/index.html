Lighting for festivals and clubs

Festivals

At a festival, lighting has to carry several stages, several artists and several moods, from early afternoon to the last set. We design the lighting of each stage with the organisers, program the shows and operate them live: a strong visual identity for every stage, and real consistency from one end of the event to the other. Technical drawings and 3D renders make it possible to validate the layout well before load-in.

Clubs and nightclubs

In a club, lighting is lived every night, as close as possible to the music and the crowd. We design lighting to suit the room and its identity, program moods and sequences on the console, then handle operation so the result stays precise and alive, night after night. A 3D render lets you see the atmosphere of the room before anything is installed.
