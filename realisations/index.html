Our work

Four ways to work with your project

Events

For an event — festival, party, concert — we handle the technical production: sound, lighting, equipment, installation, programming and operation, from load-in to load-out. One team for the whole project or for a single task.

Clubs and venues

In a club, venue or public space, we work directly on the installation: setting up lighting, sound, control and programming equipment, as well as modifying or upgrading an existing set-up and supporting technical changes over time.

Shows and programming

For a show, we design the lighting, produce the technical drawings and 3D renders, then write the programming on the console: palettes, cues and sequences timed to the music and the staging.

Equipment rental

For a one-off need or to complete your own set-up, we rent sound, lighting and backline equipment.
